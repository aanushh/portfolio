# More about me

I enjoy building software that's simple, maintainable, and solves the actual problem. I don't believe in adding complexity unless there's a clear reason for it. Sometimes the simplest solution is the right one, and sometimes investing a little more upfront makes the system much easier to maintain in the long run. Learning where to draw that line has been one of the most valuable lessons in my career.

I haven't contributed to open source yet, but it's on my mind. Soon.

Outside of work, I'm a bit of an automotive enthusiast and can happily spend hours talking about cars and bikes. I also enjoy cooking on weekends, self-hosting a few services at home, and traveling whenever I get the chance, mostly for the food and the experience.
