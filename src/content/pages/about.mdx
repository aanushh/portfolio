I believe in simple solutions. Over-engineering for the sake of it is waste, but I'll happily over-engineer the right thing if it makes the system simpler in the long run. That balance — knowing when to build and when not to — is what I've sharpened over the years.

Outside of code, I'm an automotive enthusiast who can talk about cars for hours, a weekend cook experimenting in the kitchen, and someone who self-hosts services just because I can. I also love to travel — new places, new food, new perspectives.

Right now I'm reading _Rich Dad Poor Dad_, with _The Pragmatic Programmer_ lined up next. And on the lighter side, I've never outgrown _Suppandi_ comics — sometimes the simplest humour is the best.

I haven't contributed to open source yet, but it's on my mind. Soon.
