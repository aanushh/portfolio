When we are into the coding platform, basically every second and every minute we look into either the IDE or the text editor we use (in common VS Code)

The main concept I rely on is my eye protection and appearance of the screen for long duration.

I thought the colours that we maintain to our eye will play a major role and I have selected some dark themes that fit for my eyes and my likeness.

> ***I'm not telling this must only be used or no promotion for the theme devs too 😅. It's just my POV. If you like this post and felt its worth your time, please do give claps and share it with others***
